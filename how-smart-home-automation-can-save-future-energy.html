HOW SMART HOME AUTOMATION CAN SAVE FUTURE ENERGY

 

 

After the 2 last tsunami's many started questioning and really get concerned with Environment and Global Warming issues. Although today, we Might Not be able to stop nor reduce our Life style Needs of consumable energy regardless where that energy comes from and what it cause to environment, but we are surely capable of reducing and even stopping the waste in energy that is commonly exercised daily.

Soon we can move closer to ultra-efficiency and almost Zero Wasted Energy at Homes, and office space.

Smart-BUS Patented Technology and Solution can help reduce Energy Waste enormously. It is Almost like Pay as you Use.

Before starting, we like to give few calculated examples showing how small things can save allot:

• "Do you know that switching off one Light Bulb for one hour Saves 22,000 Watts per Year?"

• "Do you know that switching off one A/C for one hour Saves 500,000 Watts per Year?"

• "Do you know that switching off electric Water Heater for one hour Saves 1,300,000.00 Watts per Year?"

Now let's see how Smart-BUS Automation does its famous Techie tricks that have proven to save allot without even affecting our way of living:

Simply Smart-BUS Occupancy Sensors Detect human movement and Activity inside the space, then S.BUS system is designed after Studying daily lifestyle of residents during normal days, holidays and weekends. Also study Cultural Habits, likings or disliking as well as Body size, Geographical location and more. (The system become like the invisible Bottler that work for you perfectly without bothering you at all)

Accordingly, this invisible Energy bottler action his plan as following:

• If No one is at a room for more than 30 Minutes and is not Visitor or party Mode then System will switch Lights off in that area cutting down wastage

• During Normal Day when daylight is enough, system will switch off garden Lights and outdoor lights like balconies and building lights, and will keep checking on hourly basis in case someone switched it on by mistake.

• During Normal Dwelling night Modes, All Lights in General Passages will be either kept off or Dimmed to Minimal, but once motion or occupancy is detected, and then automatically shall brighten up.

• One hour After We sleep All Water Boilers will be switched off except in Master Bedroom, then automatically switch on 1 Hour before Wakeup time, then switch off all water Heaters Directly after All go to school or work Except at Kitchen.

• Air-conditioning at Home shall be Switched off During School and Work hours, then Back again to the Cool/Heat set point one hour before back to home schedules, while keeping an eye always on the minimal and maximal set points.

• If we forget any Door, window open for longer time (e.g. 20 Minutes) then Air-conditioning will be turned off in that area automatically to decrease infiltration.

Also, we can simply Study other Factors that Cause Energy Wastage at Homes and office space that Smart-BUS can help us in mentoring and accordingly alert us to rectify them trying to politely help us reach Zero Wastage Environmental levels. "Energy-Smart"

a) Filters Change and cleaning alerts. (Do you know that cleaning AC filters not only Save Energy Consumption to almost half, but also create a more healthy space?)

b) Dimming of Bright Lights. (do you know that Dimming Lights Saves Electricity twice: 1-Less lighting wattage consumption, 2- less heat generation and thus less Air-conditioning is needed to overcome that heat then to cool the space, but also create a more ambient atmosphere and more elegant dwelling.

c) Properly maintaining and Sealing fully all Door and Window gasket space. (Do you know that this help in reaching total energy efficiency by not only decreasing infiltration (Energy Loss), but also to keep any Penetration of outside unprocessed air, dust or insects and thus keep a healthier dwelling)

Only some know that Smart Home BUS Creation was driven by a "Father Dream" whom wanted his son to invent something which add and help Humanity one Day. This father is not only a Poet that Love Nature, People, and Good to all, but was also a great educator that has always trained all his family on wastage Awareness since childhood. Thus, Smart-BUS Was

Created putting in mind more than 100 targets like:

i) Using Lesser wires as Possible (less wastage in Pipes, Plastics, Copper ++)
ii) Making least possible Wall Breaking (less Noise and Dust Pollution)
iii) Providing Stable high Protection system (minimizing Wasted Repair Visits)
iv) One Product is flexible to handle many tasks (minimizing Space and Plastic)

All the Above help Save energy wastage either in direct or indirect way. And Smart Home-BUS Designer and Inventor made more Methods that correlate to reach one simple Final Target: "Energy-Smart" Environment.
