SMART HOTEL CONTROL

 

 

The word "Hotel" derived from the old French word "hostel" meaning residence.
If hotel do means residence, what does it really consist of? Does it have all the home facilities? Yes actually, hotel is Serviced Bottler style home which has mainly a place to sleep, a place to Dine, to sit and relax, and perhaps a nice veranda or Garden. Some even have own suite Kitchens and Mini-bars in smaller version to that at home.

The word hotel conjures association as a "traveler's haven". A hotel is a place as well where big wedding functions and other social events is most often times being held.

 

What can Smart-BUS add to the hotel parts?

Since the Target behind Hotels is to mainly supply a comfortable serviced temporary space, and since the functionality of that space. Smart-Hotel Solution has been created to serve exactly these targeted functionalities and services pulls eye while understanding that Hotel Guest is a swift temporary resident that require uncomplicated easy to use system as following:

A - Hotel Guest Room Management System "GRMS"

Since The functionality in Guest bedroom is mainly limited to: Sleep, wakeup, Wash, Dress, watch TV, Meet some Guests. Accordingly Smart GRMS was designed to serve as following:
1 - Increase Service Speed Level (online Requests: DND, Clean, Laundry)
2 - Create Elegant Walls with minimal Switches
3 - Locate Panels accurately where it is mostly to be used (At entrance, near Study & at Bedside)
4 - Accurately Study Room Real Occupancy by detecting Humans availability within that space and matching to Card holder status
5 - Provide elegant all in one door access panel with service request
6 - Control energy consumption, wasted energy control during vacancies
7 - Monitor Vital Systems status at Hotel Guest room like HVAC, Lighting, Curtains
8 - Track House Keeping Staff Activities closely and accurately
9 - Adding to the service level, comfort and health elements during guest stay which increases business income eventually due to recommendations and recurring referrals.
10 - Centrally monitor room security and emergency assistance requests

B - Hotel Restaurant and Lobby

1 - Control of Mood Lighting according to Daylight and space or occasion
2 - Stream background Music to add to the ambiance
3 - Control internal air quality and climate for added comfort

C - Hotel Ball and Conference Rooms

1 - Control of Mood Lighting according to space function
2 - Control All Audio, visual, Presentation, and multimedia devices
3 - Control Fresh air circulation and temperature at each space
4 - Combine or Separate system controls according to space partitioning status
5 - Create the needed Show control effects according to the needed occasion like wedding or project inauguration function etc.

D - Hotel Building

• Mainly to Control building façade and architectural Lighting in dynamic way

The entire above, are included in what we call "Smart-Hotel" which is a Micro level control functionality paired to actual space functional requirement.

 

Is BMS similar to Smart-Hotel?

The Building management System "BMS" Technology which is mainly and engineering level central control and monitoring tool that differs in service level to that offered by "Smart-Hotel" solution. BMS is considered as Macro level Control.

The BMS Technology is called generally (Hotel Control) because it controls hotel systems centrally like: Elevators, Fire alarm, Lighting, Ventilation in the Public areas mainly, Pumps, Security and CCTV, Parking Management, Central battery, and Backup generators systems etc.

In short, Smart-Hotel Care about Human space Service & Micro functionality Requirement Level, while BMS Care about Engineering System Maintenance Macro Building Level.
