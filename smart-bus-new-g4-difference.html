WHY G4 (GENERATION-4) S-BUS?

 

 

Smart-Group® Research and Development Labs "Smart-LABS ®" has worked Hard for Past One Year to Face lift the Smart-BUS technology adding More Features, Making If User friendly and simple for Installer, Innovating in the Cabling Topology, and Making the Looks and Finishes at best elegance possible to pace today's Techno-lifestyle era.

Many New Devices added that are only compatible with the New G4, and were not available within the Old Smart-BUS Product Basket like:

• Ceiling fan Speed Module to control 6 Units each with 5 speeds
• Outdoor Temperature, Humidity, and Pressure sensor with Auxiliary Pool/water sensor Temp Probe
• 3Phase Surge Protection and Auto Disconnection Module
• FDP (Fixed Display Panel)
• Smart Player (Android TV Box)
• Advanced 9in1, 6in1 Sensors
• New Cable Manager (Bus Maker)
• Hotel Bedside Touch Controller
• Hotel Aux – Stand Alone Energy Link
